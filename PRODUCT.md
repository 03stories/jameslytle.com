# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is design and technology leaders considering James for senior roles, founder-level collaborations, or ambitious product work. They need to quickly understand what James can own, how independently he works, and whether the quality of his judgment and execution matches the complexity of their opportunity.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio creates qualified conversations about senior opportunities and collaborations. It should demonstrate that James can take ambiguous product problems from strategy through a high-quality working product with little oversight.

## Positioning

James is a Design Engineer and Product Design Leader who connects product strategy, interface design, production code, and AI behavior to make complex products useful.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: start a conversation with James.
- Secondary CTA: view James's résumé.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: James connects product strategy, interface design, production code, and AI behavior to make complex products useful.
- Belief ladder:
  1. James has senior product judgment.
  2. He can independently take ambiguous problems from strategy through a working product.
  3. His design, code, and AI capabilities are demonstrated by real shipped work.
  4. He would be a low-oversight, high-quality partner or hire.
- Proof on hand: existing project case studies, authentic product imagery, and client logos associated with specific projects. Create a future logo-led proof entry point. Testimonials and measurable outcomes remain content TODOs until real material is supplied; do not invent placeholder claims.

## Brand Personality

Self-directed, pragmatic, and thorough. The portfolio should feel confident and experienced without becoming self-important. Its voice should make clear that James proactively identifies and solves difficult problems, maintains high standards, and can be trusted to work independently.

The Cimen Fluid demo is a layout reference specifically for mixed image scales, multi-column pacing, and generous whitespace; it is not a wholesale visual-style reference.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS portfolios, card-heavy grids, fake terminal aesthetics, fashionable-but-empty AI imagery, and literal Japanese motifs.

## Design Principles

1. Show the work as proof through real artifacts, clear roles, and honest descriptions of what shipped.
2. Make end-to-end ownership legible, connecting strategic judgment to design decisions and production implementation.
3. Make complex ideas understandable without flattening their substance.
4. Practice pragmatic craft: quality, restraint, and detail should serve the problem rather than decorate it.
5. Earn trust quickly by being precise about contribution, outcomes, and the boundaries of each claim.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Use `axe-core` alongside the existing accessibility audit, keyboard testing, responsive verification, color-contrast checks, readable typography, semantic structure, and reduced-motion support. Automated checks supplement rather than replace manual review.
