# Portfolio Goals

## Objective

Make the portfolio credible for intentionally hybrid senior roles spanning design engineering, product design, and product leadership, especially where complex data or AI ideas need to move into production.

## Primary audience

Hiring managers and product/design leaders evaluating an intentionally hybrid senior candidate with equal product judgment, design craft, and implementation depth. The preferred company profile still needs confirmation.

## Positioning

James is a hybrid design engineer and product designer who frames, designs, and helps build complex data and AI products from idea through production.

## Portfolio promise

Within ten seconds, the portfolio should make these claims credible:

- [ ] I can frame and solve meaningful product problems.
- [ ] I demonstrate strong visual and interaction craft.
- [ ] I can prototype, build, or materially influence implementation.

## Frozen V1 scope

### Must Ship

- [ ] Home and positioning
- [ ] Three presentable major case studies
- [ ] Selected live-project links
- [ ] Four to six lab items
- [ ] Three curated writing pieces
- [ ] About
- [ ] Resume/contact
- [ ] Refreshed Home aesthetic with consistent patterns across relevant V1 routes
- [ ] Responsive, accessible, performant launch pass

### Should Improve

- [ ] Replace legacy project summaries with decision-led snapshots where evidence supports them
- [x] Refresh stale About copy and links

### Post-V1

- [ ] Additional visual polish or motion
- [ ] Archive cleanup

## Quality bar

A hiring manager can quickly understand the positioning, verify three distinct stories of senior ownership, and see shipped or tangible evidence without relying on unsupported claims.

## Target date or trigger

No date recorded. Trigger: credible enough to send with the next targeted application or introduction.
