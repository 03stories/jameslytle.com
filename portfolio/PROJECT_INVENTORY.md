## Score evidence

### Forward / Insights Recap

- Importance: Client CSMs could not consistently communicate ROI beyond top accounts; manual reporting consumed hours, long-tail customers were underserved, and retention was at risk.
- Ownership: James's firsthand account establishes ownership from concept through MVP and first proposal, then product direction for the first production release; senior-engineer boundaries are explicit.
- Decision richness: Two complete tradeoffs are captured: reject query-backed filtering to preserve narrative focus; reject unreviewed AI output in favor of AI-assisted exploration and deterministic, human-approved scaling.
- Outcome: The proposal became a paid pilot; roughly 13,000 narratives were processed and 9,772 sent after quality gating. Users showed strong sharing/repeat demand, over 60% completion among openers, and 87% annual-repeat intent among finishers. Engagement denominators are pending.
- Visual/interaction quality: One Home asset is reused in multiple placements; broader product evidence is not inventoried.
- Differentiation: Strongest visible AI, product origination, and production-build signal.
- Technical/Design Engineer signal: James owned frontend implementation, narrative AI skill design/development, and the generation pipeline for thousands of narratives.
- Unknowns affecting score: Engagement denominators, broader shipped evidence, precise QA workflow, and client-safe wording.
